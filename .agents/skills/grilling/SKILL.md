---
name: grilling
description: Ask the user clarifying questions before any significant piece of coursework work, a few at a time, and record the answers in the decisions log. Use before starting or changing a prototype, a deploy, the screencast or the submission, when a decision is not answered by the brief, the contracts or the decisions log, or when the user says "grill me".
---

Interview me about this piece of work until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions in order. For each question, give your recommended answer and one line of reasoning.

Ask a few questions at a time, three or four at most, and wait for my answers before continuing. Group questions that belong together; do not send a long list.

Before asking anything, read [`brief.md`](../../contracts/brief.md), [`decisions.md`](../../contracts/decisions.md) and the contract for the work in hand. Do not ask what they already answer.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (files, tools, the CW1 report, the brief), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine: put each one to me and wait for my answer.

When the deadline is close, say which questions can safely take your recommended answer, so I can accept them in one go.

Record every answer as a new row in [`decisions.md`](../../contracts/decisions.md) with today's date and "User" as the decider. If an answer changes a contract, update that contract in the same step. Move anything still unanswered to the open questions table with the assumption you will work under.

Do not act on the work until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
