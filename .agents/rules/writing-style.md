# Writing style

Applies to every word an agent writes here: interface text, seed data, README, captions, handoff notes, contracts.

- **British spelling.** enrolment, centre, organise, colour, behaviour, licence (noun), programme (course of study). Quotes from the brief or CW1 keep their original spelling.
- **Plain wording.** Short sentences, everyday words, no marketing tone. Say what a thing does.
- **Keep the user's edits.** When revising text the user has written or changed, keep their wording and change only what was asked. Do not rewrite it into your own style.
- **Use the CW1 terms.** Administrator, teacher, parent, student, enquiry, enrolment, group, lesson, debtors list, so'm. Do not introduce synonyms such as "learner" or "class".
- **Use the CW1 messages.** Where a screen shows an error case that CW1 defines, use the CW1 wording, for example "Mark attendance for all students before saving".
- **Formats.** Dates DD.MM.YYYY, amounts like `500,000 so'm`, phone numbers `+998 XX XXX XX XX`.
- **Three languages in the app.** English, Russian and Uzbek in the Latin alphabet (NFR-02). Documents for the user are in English.
- **Code identifiers** follow the entity names in the data model contract, in English.
